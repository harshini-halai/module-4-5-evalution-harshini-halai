# Kestrel Logistics — Customer Returns Policy

This policy covers returns of parcels delivered by Kestrel Logistics on behalf of merchant partners who use the Kestrel returns service. Merchants who run their own returns process are listed on the Kestrel website and are excluded.

Customers may return an eligible item within 30 days of the delivery date. Every return requires a Return Merchandise Authorisation number, which the customer obtains from the merchant's order page or from Kestrel customer support. Parcels that arrive without an authorisation number are sent back to the customer at the customer's cost.

Once a return is authorised, Kestrel emails a prepaid return label within 24 hours. The label is valid for 10 days from the date it is issued. If the label expires, the customer must request a new authorisation number.

Refunds are issued by the merchant, not by Kestrel. Kestrel confirms receipt of the returned parcel to the merchant within 2 business days of it reaching the returns hub, and the merchant's own refund timeline applies after that.

Perishable goods, opened personal-care items and gift cards cannot be returned through the Kestrel returns service.
