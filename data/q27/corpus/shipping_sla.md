# Kestrel Logistics — Delivery Service Level Agreement

This document sets out the delivery commitments Kestrel Logistics makes to merchant partners on its Standard and Express services. The commitments apply to serviceable pin codes only; non-serviceable pin codes are quoted separately.

Standard delivery to metro cities is committed within 2 business days of pickup. Standard delivery to tier-2 cities is committed within 4 business days of pickup, and to all other serviceable locations within 6 business days.

Express delivery is available to metro cities only and is committed for the next business day when pickup happens before 2 pm. Express carries a surcharge of 180 rupees per parcel on top of the standard rate.

If Kestrel misses the committed delivery date, the merchant receives a service credit equal to 10% of the shipping fee for that parcel. Service credits are applied to the next monthly invoice and are not paid in cash.

The service level agreement does not apply during declared force majeure events, regional bandhs or carrier strikes notified on the Kestrel status page.
