# Kestrel Logistics — Merchant Billing Disputes

Merchant partners receive one consolidated invoice per month. This document explains how to dispute a charge on that invoice.

A billing dispute must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date through the merchant dashboard. Disputes raised after 60 days are not accepted, and the invoice is treated as final.

When a dispute is upheld, Kestrel issues a credit note within 10 business days of the decision. Credit notes are adjusted against the next invoice.

Invoices are due within 15 days. Overdue amounts attract a late payment fee of 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance, calculated from the due date.
