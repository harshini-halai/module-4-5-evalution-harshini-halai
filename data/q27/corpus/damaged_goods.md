# Kestrel Logistics — Damaged Goods Claims

Kestrel Logistics compensates customers for parcels that are damaged while in Kestrel custody. Damage caused by inadequate packaging by the merchant is excluded and is handled between the customer and the merchant. A claim is opened from the order tracking page by selecting the damaged item and uploading evidence of the damage. Claims can also be opened by calling Kestrel customer support during business hours.

Every claim must include at least three photographs: the outer packaging, the item, and the shipping label. Claims without photographs are closed automatically after 48 hours.

Damaged goods claims must be filed within 7 days of delivery. A claim filed after this window is considered only if the customer still has the original carrier seal and can show it was intact on arrival. Late claims without the seal are rejected without review.

Approved claims are settled at the declared value of the item, up to a maximum of 50,000 rupees per parcel. Items with a declared value above that limit must be insured separately by the merchant.

The claims team aims to give a decision within 5 business days of receiving a complete claim.
