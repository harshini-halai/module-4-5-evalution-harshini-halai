# Kestrel Logistics — New Joiner Onboarding

Welcome to Kestrel Logistics. This guide explains what happens in your first weeks.

On day one you receive your laptop, your access card and your HR portal login. IT enables full-disk encryption and multi-factor authentication before handing over the laptop, so please bring your phone to set up the authenticator app.

Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from their own team for the first 30 days. Your buddy is your first contact for practical questions about tools, people and routines.

Your probation period is 90 days. Your manager holds a probation review in the final two weeks of that period and confirms your employment in writing.
