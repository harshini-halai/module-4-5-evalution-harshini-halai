# Kestrel Logistics — Dangerous Goods Handling

Dangerous goods are items that can endanger people, vehicles or aircraft if they are packed or handled incorrectly. Merchants must declare dangerous goods at booking; undeclared dangerous goods are returned to the merchant and the account may be suspended.

Lithium-ion batteries shipped on their own must carry the UN3480 hazard label. A parcel sent by air may contain at most 2 standalone lithium-ion batteries. Batteries installed in equipment are permitted without this limit but must be protected against accidental activation.

Aerosols, flammable liquids and compressed gases are not accepted on any Kestrel service. Perfumes are accepted by surface transport only and must be packed upright.

Hub staff who handle declared dangerous goods must hold a current dangerous goods handling certificate, renewed every 24 months.
