# Kestrel Logistics — Leave Policy (effective 1 April 2025)

This policy applies to everyone on the Kestrel Logistics payroll in India. It explains how paid leave is earned, booked and carried over. Questions about this policy should go to the People Operations team in Pune, who maintain the leave register and approve exceptions.

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Part-time employees accrue paid annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave is booked through the HR portal at least five working days in advance, except in emergencies.

Contractors engaged through a vendor agreement are not eligible for paid annual leave. Contractors may take up to 6 days of unpaid leave per contract year with the written approval of the engagement manager. Unpaid leave beyond 6 days ends the engagement unless the vendor agreement says otherwise.

New joiners serve a probation period of 90 days. Leave continues to accrue during probation, but it cannot be taken during probation except for bereavement leave. Accrued days become available on the first working day after probation ends.

Unused paid annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 6 days. Any balance above 6 days lapses on 31 March of the following year and is not paid out. Encashment of leave is permitted only at the time of resignation, and only for the carried-forward balance.

Bereavement leave of 5 paid days is available to all employees, including those on probation, on the death of an immediate family member. Bereavement leave does not reduce the annual leave balance.
