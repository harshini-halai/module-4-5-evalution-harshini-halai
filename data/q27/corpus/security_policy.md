# Kestrel Logistics — Information Security Policy

This policy protects customer data, merchant data and Kestrel systems. It applies to employees, contractors and vendors with access to Kestrel systems.

Passwords for all Kestrel accounts must be changed every 90 days and must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal and every system that stores customer data.

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 4 hours of discovery, using the incident form or the security hotline. Do not attempt to investigate an incident yourself before reporting it.

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Laptops must use full-disk encryption, which IT enables before a laptop is issued.
