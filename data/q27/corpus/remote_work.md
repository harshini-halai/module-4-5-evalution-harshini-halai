# Kestrel Logistics — Remote Work Policy

Kestrel Logistics supports a hybrid working model for office-based roles. Warehouse, fleet and hub operations roles are site-based and are not covered by this policy. Managers remain accountable for team coverage on every working day.

Confirmed employees in office-based roles may work from home on Fridays without separate approval. Working from home on any other weekday requires approval from the reporting manager, recorded in the HR portal before the day begins.

Employees on probation may work from home only with written approval from their reporting manager for each day requested. Probationers should expect most requests to be declined during their first 30 days, when onboarding sessions are held in person.

Employees who work from home receive a one-time home-office equipment stipend of 15,000 rupees, paid with the first salary after the employee's first approved remote day. The stipend is not repeated if the employee changes teams.

Remote workers must connect through the company VPN and must not use public Wi-Fi networks for any system that holds customer data. Security requirements in the Information Security Policy apply in full when working remotely.
