# Kestrel Logistics — Travel and Expense Policy

Employees travelling on company business are reimbursed for reasonable expenses that follow this policy. Travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager in the expense system before booking.

The daily allowance for meals and local transport is 2,500 rupees in metro cities and 1,600 rupees elsewhere. The daily allowance is paid without receipts.

Any individual expense above 500 rupees outside the daily allowance must be supported by an itemised receipt. Expense claims must be submitted within 45 days of the expense date; claims submitted later are rejected unless the Finance Controller approves an exception.

Economy class is the default for flights. Business class may be booked only for flights longer than 6 hours and only with approval from a department head.
